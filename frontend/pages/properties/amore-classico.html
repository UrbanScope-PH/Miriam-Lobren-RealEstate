About This Property

Amore Classico by Benedetto Residences is a thoughtfully designed three-storey townhouse ideal for growing families who need more space, comfort, and functionality. Located in Barangay Pulang Lupa Uno, Las Piñas City, this property offers a practical home layout designed to accommodate the different needs of every family member.

With a 52 sqm lot area and 102 sqm total developed floor area, the property features three bedrooms, two toilets and baths, one powder room, a dedicated maid's room, a family hall, and a patio.

One of its standout features is its three-storey design, which maximizes the available living space while providing separate areas for relaxation, family bonding, and everyday activities. The property is also Ready for Occupancy (RFO), making it a great option for homebuyers looking for a home they can move into without waiting for the completion of construction.

Available Ready for Occupancy Units: Block 1B, Lot 6 · Block 1B, Lot 7 · Block 2B, Lot 8.
