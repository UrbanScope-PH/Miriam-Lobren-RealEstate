About This Property

Benecia Single Attached offers a modern and spacious home designed for families looking for comfort, privacy, and functionality. Located in Sihanouk Village, Barangay Talon IV, Las Piñas City, this property provides a practical residential setting with convenient access to the surrounding establishments and main roads of the city.

With approximately 102 sqm of lot area and 117 sqm of floor area, the house is designed to maximize living space while providing separate areas for relaxation and everyday family activities. Its features include spacious living and kitchen areas, bedrooms, a maid's room, a balcony, and a lanai.

One of its standout features is its single-attached design, offering a more private residential setup compared with typical townhouse units. It also comes with a carport and a front fence and gate, providing added convenience and privacy for homeowners. Benecia is a great option for growing families looking for a modern, functional, and spacious home in Las Piñas City.
