About the Property

Midori Mid-Rise Condo – Zone 1 offers a convenient and comfortable living experience for young professionals, couples, and starting families looking for an affordable home in Dasmariñas, Cavite.

With a 50 sqm floor area, each unit features two bedrooms, a toilet and bath, a living area, a dining and kitchen area, and a patio. Buyers can choose between Inner Units and End Units, depending on their preferences and lifestyle.

Strategically located near Dasmariñas Bayan, schools, and essential establishments, the development also provides convenient access to public transportation.

Midori offers a practical and comfortable living space where residents can enjoy privacy, convenience, and a place to create lasting memories with their families.
