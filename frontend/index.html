Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, reserving, and financing your dream home with CD Miranda Realty.

To start your application, please prepare the following initial documents:

  • 2 Valid Government IDs
  • Latest 1-month Payslip & Proof of Income
  • Certificate of Employment (COE)
  • Proof of Billing
  • TIN (Tax Identification Number)
  • Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card or Member's Data Form (MDF)
  • 1x1 ID Picture (can be submitted to follow)

Disclaimer: Pag-IBIG documents (Loyalty Card/MDF) are collected strictly for applicant identification and documentation purposes. CD Miranda Realty does not handle or process Pag-IBIG financing transactions.

We offer structured payment channels for your reservation fee, monthly down payment (equity), and loan amortization, including:

  • Direct bank deposit or electronic bank transfer to official developer accounts
  • Post-dated checks (PDCs) for scheduled monthly equity settlements
  • Accredited payment centers and online developer merchant facilities
  • Auto-debit arrangements (ADA) with your designated financing bank

*Official receipts (ORs) are issued directly by accredited property developers for all received payments.

Our accredited subdivisions and communities are strategically situated near major expressways and arterial road networks:

  • Rapid transit connectivity via MCX, CAVITEX, CALAX, and Aguinaldo Highway
  • Accessible public transportation including UV Express commuter vans, modern jeepneys, buses, and local tricycle terminals
  • Convenient proximity to schools, tertiary hospitals, shopping malls, and civic centers

*Specific transportation routes, terminal locations, and travel schedules may vary by individual property subdivision.

Yes, you may apply with a co-borrower if your individual income does not meet the required threshold. Your co-borrower will simply need to submit the same set of standard identity and income requirements.

Yes, qualified OFWs can apply for bank financing. Because OFWs typically do not have a local Certificate of Employment (COE), you must provide:

  • Valid Passport and Visa
  • Overseas Employment Contract (from the DMW; must have at least 1 year validity and be translated into English if written in another language)
  • Flight tickets and arrival/departure travel records
  • Special Power of Attorney (SPA) / locally notarized authorization for your representative in the Philippines
  • Proof of income, bank statements, and formal job description

*Note: Not all OFWs automatically qualify for bank financing. Approval is subject to bank evaluation, as certain properties and banks require specific job descriptions (often prioritizing white-collar roles).

Yes, Virtual Assistants are eligible to purchase a property. You will need to provide your bank statements along with an active Job Order or Employment Contract showing at least a 1-year contract duration.

Yes, seafarers (including ship captains and maritime officers) can apply. Required documents include bank statements, an active Employment Contract or Job Order, proof of income, and standard maritime employment records (subject to final bank accreditation guidelines).

Yes, legally married but separated individuals can still purchase a property. However, you will need to provide either signed marital consent from your spouse or a valid court order (such as an annulment decree or legal separation document) to legally establish your marital status.
